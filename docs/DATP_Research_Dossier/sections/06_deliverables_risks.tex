\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\section{Risks and Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item N-BaIoT has only 9 physical devices.
  \item B4 is exploratory at small K.
  \item CICIoT2023 file-level clients are not physical-device clients.
  \item $E=1$ limits local drift.
  \item Five seeds limit statistical power.
  \item No adversarial robustness evaluation.
  \item No privacy mechanism.
  \item No hardware or deployment validation.
  \item No concept drift modeling.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}%

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\section{Governance}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Keep the main claim centered on Regime A B1 vs.\ B2 CV(FPR).
  \item Keep B0 outside the controlled threshold ladder.
  \item Keep B3 Regime A only.
  \item Treat Regime B as an applicability boundary.
  \item Treat Regime C as secondary severity evidence.
  \item Do not claim universal personalization benefit.
  \item Do not claim privacy or deployment readiness.
  \item Do not mix DATP with DATP-CP poisoning claims.
  \item Do not absorb journal-extension goals.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
